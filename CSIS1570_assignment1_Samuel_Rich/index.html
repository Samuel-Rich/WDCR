Peak

Peak is a game about climbing to the top of a mountain. You will have to navigate through different biomes, each with their own unique challenges and enemies. Along the way, you will find items that will help you on your journey. The ultimate goal is to reach the peak and call for rescue, but there are also hidden areas and secrets to discover.
