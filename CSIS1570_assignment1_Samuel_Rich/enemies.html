The enemies of Peak

There are many enemies in the game that you will encounter as you progress through the different biomes. I will briefly show them here.

In alphabetical order

Antlion

The Antlion is a creature that lies in wait inside the sand dunes of the Mesa. Inside these dunes is a fake luggage case to lure you in, and when you get close enough, the Antlion grabs you with its mandibles.

Beetle

The Beetle resides in the Roots, and will headbutt you if you get too close, sending you flying.

Big Ghost

The Big Ghost can be found in the Gloom. It will spawn nearby on a cooldown and will explode if it gets close enough to you, sending you flying and applying a large stack of cold to you. It can be burned to excorcise it.

Frog

The Frog can also be found in the Gloom. If you get close enough, it will grab you with its tongue and pull you in on a cooldown. You can pickup the frog and cook it for food.

Scorpion

The Scorpion can be found in the Mesa. It will sting you if you get too close, applying a poison effect. You can quickly pick it up and throw it off the mountain.

Scoutmaster Myers

Scoutmaster Myers is a hostile enemy you will rarely see in any biome. He only appears if you break rule 0: Never leave a friend behind. If you push too far ahead of your friends, he will chase you and throw you off the mountain. He can also be summoned with the Scoutmaster's Bugle.

Spider

The Spider can be found in the Roots. It hangs from random ledges and branches and will capture you in a web cacoon if you wander too close directly below it.

Zombie

The Zombie can also be found in the roots. It will be laying in one guaranteed spot each day, and will wake up if you get in a close proximity to it. It will chase you and attempt to bite you, infecting you if it succeeds. You can use a medkit to cure the infection.
